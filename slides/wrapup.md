# Should you switch?

## A pragmatic take

- **Yes, today:** notes, slides, reports, theses (with kinetic-kit)
- **Maybe wait:** journals mandating LaTeX, heavy legacy-macro workflows
- **Migration:** keep your `.bib`, move incrementally, no all-or-nothing
